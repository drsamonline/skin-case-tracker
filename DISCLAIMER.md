## What this app is

**Skin-Case Tracker** records a skin case over time — History, Habits, Mapping and Symptoms — and reads the 90-day pattern back as a chart. It is the companion app to *The Skin Is a Liar*.

It **records; it does not diagnose.** It is not a medical device, and it does not identify a condition, recommend a treatment or tell you whether a lesion is benign.

## The pattern is not a diagnosis

A correlation between a habit and a flare over ninety days is an observation about one person's notes. It is not evidence of cause, and it is not a clinical finding. The app shows the pattern you logged; it does not interpret it.

## Skin signs that need a doctor now

Some skin changes are serious and time-critical. See a doctor promptly if a mole or lesion:

- changes shape, colour or size, or becomes asymmetrical;
- bleeds, itches or crusts without healing;
- appears new and grows quickly;
- is accompanied by fever, spreading redness, or feeling unwell.

**In an emergency, call 108 (India) or your local emergency number.**

Nothing in this app is a reason to delay that.

## Responsibility

Clinical decisions remain with the qualified practitioner treating the patient. The accuracy of a record is the responsibility of the person who entered it — the app stores what you type and does not check it.
