The HHMS Skin-Case Tracker is a working implementation of the HHMS dashboard in The Skin Is a Liar (Dr. Sohil Momin, MATNEO, 2026), chapters 11 and 14. It records History, Habits, Mapping and Symptoms, reads them as a 90-day pattern, and runs the 30/60/90-day audit loop.

It records; it does not diagnose. Nothing here is medical advice. The reading of the pattern is the clinician's job. All data stays in this browser and never leaves your device.
